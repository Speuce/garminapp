Know what comes next.

Three ways to set an alarm.

Which one feels like your JumpStart?

01 / Trail — list & wizard

Familiar and guided. Select an alarm from a list, then work through time and repeat settings. A visible selection bar keeps your place. Easy to learn; more steps for a small change.

02 / Orbit — cards & dial

Big, deliberate decisions. Browse one full-screen alarm at a time. UP / DOWN turn a virtual dial or cycle through one pattern at a time. The clearest focus; less information visible at once.

03 / Roster — agenda & field editor

For seeing your week at a glance. Start with actual upcoming dates, jump straight to a setting, and scan a weekday grid. Efficient edits and more context; denser screens require more attention.
